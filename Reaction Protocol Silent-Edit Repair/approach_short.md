The first thing I did was line up the train and test notes side by side, and they looked nothing alike: training notes were clean and fully labeled, but test notes were unlabeled, showed only three of the six operations, and used bench-tag suffixes I'd never seen before. I realized the real signal lived in each tag's word-prefix, not the digits. So I fine-tuned a T5 model, but trained it on degraded copies of every training row that imitate the messy test format. That taught it to decode the tags, apply the correction notice, and infer the missing slots itself.
