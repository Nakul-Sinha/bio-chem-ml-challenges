I dug into the data first and found the catalysts and solvents are stripped out of the reaction SMILES, so I really had to infer conditions from the reaction structure itself. I built Morgan fingerprints of the reactants, products, and their difference, plus flags for common reagents, then trained one multi-task network to predict all four targets at once. The trick that moved the needle was framing solvent as a softmax "primary solvent" choice instead of independent yes/no labels, since most reactions use a single solvent. Finally I tuned the decision thresholds straight against the composite metric, especially for the rare classes.
