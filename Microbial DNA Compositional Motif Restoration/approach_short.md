Validation tripped me up first. Train and test are different genomes, so I had to split my CV by genome or I'd just be kidding myself about the score. My first co-occurrence model came in under the baseline, which bugged me, so I dug into why — and it turned out the masked k-mer actually sits in one of the sibling contigs about 94% of the time. So finding it wasn't really the problem; ranking it was. What finally clicked was treating it like a recommendation problem: for each contig I let its most-similar sibling contigs vote on the missing token, trusting the rarer shared k-mers more and playing down the ones that turn up in basically every contig. I did try a small transformer too, but it just memorized the training genomes and fell apart on unseen ones, so I stuck with the count-based version.
