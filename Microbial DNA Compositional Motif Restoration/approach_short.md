Since train and test come from completely different genomes, the first thing I fixed was my validation: I split by genome so I was always testing on unseen ones. The big realization was that I could pool each genome's other contigs to estimate its own k-mer co-occurrence, and the masked token usually shows up in those siblings. So I rank candidates mainly by how strongly they co-occur with the visible tokens within that specific genome, backed off with global co-occurrence and a genome-specificity term. I also caught a sneaky leak and made the genome stats use visible tokens only. A small transformer overfit the genomes, so I kept it count-based.
